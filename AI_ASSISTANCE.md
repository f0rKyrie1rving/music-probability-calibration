# Research and development assistance

This project was initiated under Chenglin Song's direction as a separate
probability-calibration study following the Music Classification application.
Generative AI assisted with literature lookup, experimental design, implementation,
tests, numerical verification and drafting the documentation.

Reported values are computed from the bundled, provenance-checked source-derived
data. No generated audio, invented labels or fabricated experimental observations
are used. Old results and evaluation exposure are explicitly retained. This is
an exploratory research prototype; AI assistance and passing software checks do
not establish scientific novelty, independent replication or publication readiness.
